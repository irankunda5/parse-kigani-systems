Put your class schedule on your calendar

Reads your registered courses straight from Banner — with rooms and instructors — and gives you a calendar file you can import anywhere.

Schedule → Calendar

Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar. Don't click it here — it only works on Banner.

Bookmarks bar hidden? Ctrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.

Using it

  1. Sign in to Banner and open your class schedule so the weekly grid is on screen.
  2. Click the Schedule → Calendar bookmark.
  3. Check the courses it found, then download the .ics file.
  4. Import that file into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.
Import into a new calendar, not your main one. In Google Calendar, create a calendar called something like “Classes” first, then import into it. If anything looks wrong you can delete the whole calendar in one step, rather than removing a semester of events by hand.

Where the dates come from

Meeting patterns, rooms, instructors and term dates all come from Banner's own records, so they match the registrar rather than anything we've guessed.

One caveat: Banner reports the term as running through the end of the exam period, not the last day of classes. Events will therefore run a couple of weeks past your final lecture. You can shorten this with the Classes end box before downloading.

Privacy

Everything happens inside your browser, on the Banner page itself. Your schedule is never uploaded, and we have no server that could receive it. The only thing your browser requests from us is the script that does the work.

If it doesn't work

  • “Your Banner session has expired” — reload the page, sign in, click the bookmark again.
  • “This does not look like a Banner registration page” — you're on another tab or page; go back to your schedule.
  • Nothing happens — make sure you dragged the button to your bookmarks bar rather than copying the page's address.
